1Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Learn Trading With Munaaf (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information when you visit our website, see or click our advertisements on Facebook, Instagram or other Meta platforms, message us, or join our trading community or Telegram/WhatsApp channels.

By using our website or contacting us, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use our services or submit your information.

2Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Name, mobile or WhatsApp number and email address, when you fill a form, message us or ask to join.
  • Details you choose to share, such as your trading experience, interests and questions you ask us.
  • Messages and conversations with us on email, phone, WhatsApp or Telegram.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, operating system, language and screen size.
  • IP address, approximate location (city or region level) and pages visited.
  • How you reached us (for example, which ad you clicked) and actions you take, such as clicking a button or submitting a form.

We never ask for your bank login, card PIN, OTP, UPI PIN or passwords. If anyone claiming to be us asks for these, do not share them and tell us at once.

3How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and give you the information, updates or learning material you asked for.
  • To add you to our community or channel and to contact you about it.
  • To measure how our website and ads perform and to improve them.
  • To show our ads to people who may be interested, and to measure those ads.
  • To prevent fraud, spam and misuse, and to follow applicable laws.

We only use your information for the purposes above, or for a purpose that is clearly related and that you would reasonably expect.

4Meta Pixel, cookies and advertising

Our website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms, Inc. (and Meta Platforms Ireland Limited). It uses cookies and similar technologies to record actions you take on our site, such as viewing a page or submitting a form.

We use this data to measure and improve our ads, to build audiences of people who may be interested in our content, and to show our ads on Facebook, Instagram and other Meta products. Meta receives this data and handles it under its own Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Change what ads you see through Meta Ad Preferences.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may work less well if you do.
  • Opt out of interest-based ads through Your Online Choices or your device’s ad settings.

We do not use the Pixel to collect sensitive information such as passwords, payment card numbers or government ID numbers, and we do not target ads using sensitive personal characteristics.

5Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as website hosting (GitHub Pages), Meta (ads and analytics), Telegram and WhatsApp (messaging), and email providers. They may use your data only to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons, when required by law, court order or a valid request from a government authority, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.
  • Business changes, if our business is merged or transferred. We would tell you if your data is affected.

6How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, or as the law requires. When you ask us to delete your data, we delete or anonymise it within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days, unless we must keep a record by law.

7Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information, such as restricted access and secure connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage on the internet is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Please keep your own accounts and devices protected.

8Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your personal information.
  • Withdraw your consent, or object to how we use your information, at any time.
  • Stop receiving messages from us. Reply “STOP” or tell us on the contact details below.

How to request deletion

Email tradewithus@gmail.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”, and include the name and phone number or email you used with us. We may ask you to confirm it is you before we act. You can also call us on 8789654346.

If you want to remove data held by Meta, use your Facebook or Instagram settings under “Off-Facebook activity” or “Your activity outside Meta technologies”.
